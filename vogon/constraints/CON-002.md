# CON-002 — The approver of a record may not be its author

A record is approved by someone other than the person who wrote it. Where
VOGON reports on approval state, it reports an approval by the record's author
as a defect rather than as coverage.

The separation is the reason the roles exist. One person may hold several
roles, subject to quality being independent of the people building the system.

**Example.** A requirement drafted by an engineer and approved in the tracker
by the same engineer is listed as an independence failure, regardless of the
issue's state.
