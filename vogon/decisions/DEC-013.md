# DEC-013 — The risk level is drafted with its reasoning and approved in the host project

**Context.** Every requirement carries `gxp_risk`, and that value decides how
much verification and evidence the requirement needs. A value set too low
quietly removes work everywhere downstream, and a person signing a record is
signing all of it, with no moment where that one field is examined on its own.

**Options.**

Leave `gxp_risk` absent in a draft so a person has to fill it. An absent field
is visible where a plausible wrong one is not, and it contradicts the rule
that a drafted record is `proposed` until a person accepts it, which already
covers every other field. It also adds work, which is the opposite of what
VOGON is for.

Draft the value like any other field, and give the judgement its own moment
in the risk assessment the host project produces.

**Decision.** The agent drafts `gxp_risk` with the rest of the record, and the
person who accepts the record accepts that value with it. The field carries no
separate attribution. The risk assessment is produced in the host project by
the skill VOGON installs there: for each requirement it states the level and
the reasoning behind it, with links to the records, held documents and
findings that informed it, and a person in the host project approves it. VOGON
writes no risk assessment of its own.

**Consequences.** A reviewer accepting a record sees a proposed level rather
than an empty field, which is the weaker position and is accepted because the
assessment is where the levels are examined together. VOGON has to check that
the assessment and the records still agree, because a level changed after
approval leaves the assessment asserting something nobody approved.

**Example.** A requirement is drafted with `gxp_risk: data integrity`. The
reviewer accepts the record. The risk assessment later states that level with
the constraint and the held procedure behind it, and the quality lead approves
it. Raising the level afterwards reports the assessment as out of date.
