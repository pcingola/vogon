# DEC-022 — A drafted record, plan or document is checked by a second subagent before a person sees it

**Context.** Steps that produce a record, a plan or a document produce text a
person then reviews. Each step's reference file is a checklist the output
meets or fails, and `vogon check` reports what can be checked mechanically.
An agent that checks its own draft reads it with the reasoning that produced
it, and passes what it meant rather than what it wrote.

**Options.**

The drafting agent checks its own output. One agent and no extra cost, with
the weakness above.

A second subagent checks the draft, and the loop between the two runs until
the checker reports nothing. Two agents that disagree about a judgement can
loop without end.

A second subagent checks the draft, the loop runs until the checker reports
nothing, and it stops early and hands the remaining findings to the person
after a fixed number of rounds or when a finding repeats.

**Decision.** `vogon-writer` drafts the output. `vogon-checker` applies the
checklist in the step's reference file and runs `vogon check`, and its
findings go back to the writer. The loop ends when the checker reports
nothing. After five rounds, or when the checker reports a finding the writer
has already been given, the remaining findings go to the person. Test cases
use their own pair, `vogon-test-writer` and `vogon-test-checker`, which read
only the records and the tests (`DEC-019`).

**Consequences.** A person reviewing the output does not spend the review on
findings a checklist catches. Each draft costs at least two agent runs. A
finding the writer cannot resolve reaches the person after at most five
rounds, stated as a finding. The checker is a language model, so a draft that
passes it is still reviewed by the person, and its report is not validation
evidence (`FACT-005`).

**Example.** A requirement is drafted with an acceptance block that states no
expected value. The checker reports it, the writer adds the value, and the
second check reports nothing. A decision whose options the writer cannot
describe without the design conversation is reported twice with the same
finding, and goes to the person after the second round.
