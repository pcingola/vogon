# FACT-005 — GAMP 5 treats software producing validation evidence as needing its own assessment

GAMP 5 expects a tool used to produce validation evidence to carry evidence of
its own fitness for that use, in proportion to what the tool decides. A tool
that drafts text a person then reads and approves is assessed as a supplier; a
tool whose output is offered as evidence without human review is held to the
standard of the evidence it produces.

**Example.** A spreadsheet that calculates a release criterion is assessed
before its numbers are used. A spreadsheet used to lay out a meeting agenda is
not.
