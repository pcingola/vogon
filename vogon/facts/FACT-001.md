# FACT-001 — An electronic signature requires the signer to re-enter their credentials

Under 21 CFR Part 11 and EU Annex 11, an electronic signature is applied by the
signer entering their own credentials at the moment of signing. What is stored
against that version of the record is the signer's printed name, the date and
time, and the meaning of the signature: authored, reviewed or approved.

A typed name, a pasted image of a signature, and a commit attributed to an
author are none of them electronic signatures. A signature cannot be carried
forward onto a later edit of the record it was applied to.

**Example.** A requirement issue is approved in the tracker on the 3rd. The
stored signature covers the text as it stood on the 3rd. Editing the text on
the 5th leaves a signature that no longer describes anything anyone signed.
