# VOGON brand and voice

The voice of the marketing page, `src/html/index.html`, and of nothing else.
It does not apply to the README, to CLI output, to the developer
documentation, to any record under `gxp/`, or to anything VOGON generates in a
user's project. All of those follow the writing standard in
`src/docs/dev/writing.md`.

VOGON's culture is deadpan corporate satire: bureaucracy is sacred, developers
are a regrettable implementation detail, every problem can be solved with more
process, and good enough is not compliant.

## Tone

- Deadpan. Never wink at the joke, never explain it.
- Institutional, not personal: forms, revisions, procedures, sign-offs.
- Roughly 80% of any public-facing text reads as a legitimate GxP tool. The
  remaining 20% is where it goes wrong. Lead serious, then deteriorate.
- Reuse the copy in `copy.md` and `ascii-art.md` rather than inventing new
  catchphrases.

## Fixed strings

- Tagline: *Because good enough is not compliant.*
- Banner keywords: COMPLIANCE · TRACEABILITY · RISK MANAGEMENT · A BRIGHTER TOMORROW.
- Recurring lines: "Same problems. More process." · "It depends." · "Per SOP." · "REJECTED."
