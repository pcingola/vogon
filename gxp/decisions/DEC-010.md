# DEC-010 — A summary is held beside its transcript and is never the authority

**Context.** Transcripts are long and are read once. A summary makes the
content usable, and it is produced by a model, so it is wrong in ways that read
well.

**Options.**

Summarise and discard the transcript. The evidence goes with it, and a citation
then rests on text nobody can check against what was said.

Hold the summary and treat it as the source. Convenient, and it makes a
generated document the thing a record is checked against.

Hold both, and rank them.

**Decision.** The summary is held in `gxp/sources/` beside the transcript, with
`kind: summary` and `derived_from` naming it. A `source` list naming a meeting
names the transcript; the summary may follow it and may not replace it.

**Consequences.** Someone checking a record reads the passage in the
transcript, using the summary to find it. A summary that disagrees with its
transcript is a defect in the summary and changes no record. Both files are
kept forever, which is cheap for text.
